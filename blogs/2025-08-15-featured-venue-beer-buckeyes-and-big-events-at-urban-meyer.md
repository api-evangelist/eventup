In this edition of our Venue Spotlight series, we’re taking you inside one of the most spirited spots in Ohio: Urban Meyer’s Pint House . Known for its energetic atmosphere, elevated pub fare, and deep ties to Ohio State football, Urban Meyer’s Pint House has become a go-to destination for fans and locals alike. We sat down with their team to get the inside scoop on what makes this venue stand out, how they plan unforgettable event experiences, and what it takes to run events in a space where Buckeye pride is always on full display.
