We love highlighting the people and places that make the hospitality industry thrive. In this edition of our Venue Spotlight series, we’re chatting with Amanda Kantor of Saucy Brew Works , a bold and energetic brewery with two locations known for its creative beers, inventive menus, and community-focused vibe. From coordinating small private parties at their Pinecrest location to managing large gatherings at the Cleveland location, the Saucy Brew Works team knows how to blend fun with flawless execution.
