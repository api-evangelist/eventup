Nestled in the historic Peters Cartridge Factory, a former ammunition plant, Cartridge Brewing is more than just a brewery. Known for its unique blend of industrial charm and modern hospitality, Cartridge Brewing offers a memorable setting for private events of all kinds. We sat down with their team to learn what sets this venue apart, how they manage ever-changing event trends, and why guests keep coming back for both the beer and the experience.
